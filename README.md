### ✨ The short version

I'm Charis. I build products at the intersection of technology and fun, and I'm learning the engineering in public while I do it.

The serious part is the product. The goofy part is allowed to stay.

### 🚀 Currently building

**[ZURI](https://zuriqo.com/)** is digital ordering and analytics for restaurants, shops, and events.

A guest opens a link or scans a code, picks from the menu, and pays. The venue runs orders, reservations, and payouts from one dashboard. Less clipboard. More "your order is on the way." 🍽️
